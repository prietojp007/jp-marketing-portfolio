About

Digital marketing, done end to end

I'm JP, a Digital Marketing student at UW Oshkosh managing day-to-day marketing for four programs — School of Business, MBA, Sales Program, and School of Education & Human Services — working directly with each program's leadership.

I run social media across Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn (alumni spotlights, student features, event coverage, new program launches), write the Business Bulletin — a Constant Contact newsletter reaching 1,500+ business students — and ran a Meta ad campaign. With admin access to UWO's website, I build and edit pages in WordPress and Divi Builder, and contributed to the content migration to UWO's new site. On social, I also shoot photography and edit video content, plus design supporting materials like banners and flyers.

I'm also exploring AI in marketing — building websites with AI-assisted tools and developing agents geared toward marketing and optimization.

Certifications: Digital Marketing (Skillshare/Skukent), Social Media (HubSpot), and a Property and Casualty insurance license from Erie Insurance.
